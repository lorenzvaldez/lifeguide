Why I Built This

This is personal.

"When I was younger I started down the path of nursing school. I wanted to help people — that was always the thing. But it wasn't the right fit. I went into digital marketing and eventually helped build the largest business networking organization in Hawaii."

"Over the years I did a lot of things. But nothing felt truly meaningful. Then I discovered AI and realized I was a builder. And I started thinking about my wife."

"My wife is a hospice nurse. Her best friend is a hospice nurse. My aunties are nurses. I grew up with a lot of death in my family. And I watched my wife come home from shifts describing families who were completely lost. No roadmap. No checklist. Just Google at 2am and panic."

How It Works

What a family experiences.

01

They land on thelifeguide.app

A family member — usually an adult child in crisis — finds LifeGuide searching for help. The landing page meets them where they are: "When someone you love is declining." No jargon. No clinical language. Just calm, clear next steps.

Free to start
02

They answer 4 questions about their situation

A short quiz identifies where the family is in their journey — parent declining, terminal diagnosis, hospice referral, or already in hospice. Their role in the situation. Their urgency level. What they need most right now. LifeGuide uses these answers to personalize everything.

Personalized from minute one
03

They receive their First Week Guide — free

A personalized, situation-specific guide with immediate action steps for today, this week, and beyond. Includes a preview of the 5 critical documents every family needs. This is their first taste of clarity in what may be the most chaotic time of their lives.

No credit card required
04

They unlock full access — 7 features, one place

For $20/month or $97 for 6 months, families get complete access to all 7 features. Doctor Visit Prep AI, Document Vault, Family Coordination Hub, Stage by Stage Guide, The Final Days Guide, After — The First 30 Days, and the new 2AM Caregiver Companion powered by Google Gemini.

$97 one-time · most popular
05

They have a companion for the whole journey

The 2AM Companion is always there. Families can ask anything — "what does terminal lucidity mean," "how do I coordinate getting a hospital bed delivered," "what happens to Medicare after death." Real answers. No panic-Googling. Powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash in production today.

Available 24/7 · Gemini-powered
