From the article

"I saw a big gap where families are lost in the health system. They don't know what to ask, who to call or where to go for care. AI can close the access gap where there is currently no support and a timely access problem, not a clinical one."

— Lorenz Valdez, Founder, LifeGuide

"Any tool that can improve awareness and potential access to hospice would be huge. It's the ability to streamline that process so that a loved one, a caregiver feels empowered, because they are then making decisions from a place of calm and not panic."

— Dr. Brian Haas, National Medical Director of Hospice, Ascend Health

About LifeGuide

LifeGuide is a family care navigator for hospice and end-of-life care, built to close the gap families face when navigating a fragmented health system. Features include stage-by-stage guidance, a document vault, family coordination tools, doctor visit prep, and the 2AM Caregiver Companion — a Gemini-powered AI tool offering real-time guidance on hospice logistics, documentation, and care coordination at any hour.
