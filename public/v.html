What It Is
A free app for hospice families

LifeGuide helps families going through hospice or end-of-life care with a loved one. It tells them what documents they need, what to ask the doctor, and has an AI chat they can use anytime, even at 2am.

It's free for families to start. No credit card, no catch.

Who It's For
Our target

You're not looking for sick patients or grieving families directly. You're reaching the people who already work with them every day — and asking them to pass it along.

🕊️ Hospice social workers

Staff at hospice organizations who hand families resource lists.

🏥 Hospital discharge planners

Staff who help patients leave the hospital and get set up with hospice care.

Flyers
How and who to give them to

Drop them off at hospice offices and hospital discharge departments. Nathan Adelson Hospice is a great first stop. At hospitals, ask the front desk for "discharge planning" — that's the right department.

Say this
"Hi, I help with a free app for hospice families called LifeGuide. It's free, no credit card needed. Can I leave a flyer for your team?"
Email
Who to email

Same two groups — search for hospice agencies or hospitals near you, and look for a general email like info@ or a social worker's name on their staff page.

Copy and send
Subject: A free resource for the families you work with

Hi, my name is V. I help spread the word about LifeGuide, a free app for hospice families — it helps with paperwork, doctor questions, and has a 24/7 AI chat. Completely free to use. Thought it might be helpful for the families you work with. You can check it out at thelifeguide.app. Thanks for everything you do.
