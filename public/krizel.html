What LifeGuide is

Everything families need —
in one place.

Who it's for: Adult children who suddenly find themselves responsible for a parent in decline. Spouses navigating a terminal diagnosis. Families who just received a hospice referral and have no idea what happens next. Anyone who has ever Googled "what do I do when someone is dying" at 2am.

What it does: LifeGuide walks families through the entire hospice and end-of-life journey step by step — from the very first conversation with a doctor, through active hospice care, all the way through the first 30 days after loss. It answers the questions families are too overwhelmed to know how to ask.

How it works: Families answer 4 quick questions about their situation. LifeGuide personalizes everything to their exact stage — whether they're just starting to notice decline, just entered hospice, or in the final days. They get a free First Week Guide instantly, then unlock full access for $20/month or $97 for 6 months.

What makes it different: This isn't a static PDF or a pamphlet. It's an AI-powered, interactive navigator built specifically for this journey. Two AI models work together — Claude for structured guidance, Google Gemini for real-time conversation. No health data collected. No medical records. Just a calm, private place for families to find clarity when everything feels like chaos.
